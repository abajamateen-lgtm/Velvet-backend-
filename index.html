Crawler Connection

MapMiner's search results come from your own local crawler.js server. Point this at wherever it's running.

Running in Termux? Start it with node crawler.js in the crawler folder, then leave this as http://127.0.0.1:5000. Running the crawler on another device on your network? Use that device's LAN IP instead, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:5000.
